## Google Play Ratings and Reviews: What's the Difference?

Although ratings and reviews are closely related, they serve different purposes.

**Google Play Ratings**

Ratings are numerical evaluations, usually represented by a star rating from one to five. They provide a quick overview of how users perceive an application.

**Google Play Reviews**

Reviews are written comments submitted by users. They provide additional details about an application's features, performance, and usability.

Both are important parts of an app's public feedback profile.

Developers should focus on improving the overall user experience rather than attempting to manipulate either metric.
